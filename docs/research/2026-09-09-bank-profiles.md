# Bank profiles research (input for ticket #21)

Three research passes, one per group of institutions, checked 2026-09-09. Every fact carries its
source; every unverified fact is flagged inline. This file is research, not data: the `institutions`
and `bank-profiles` datasets are written in ticket #21 only after the owner approves the proposed
scores.

## Gaps that need a human before the data is trusted

- **FGC conglomerate membership** could not be read from fgc.org.br (interactive page). It drives
  the reserve's FGC-headroom math; verify by hand per institution.
- **Reclame Aqui** blocks automated reads (HTTP 403) and **consumidor.gov.br** is JS-rendered: every
  review figure below comes from search snippets and is unverified.
- **App-store ratings** for Inter and Itaú could not be pulled live.
- **Bradesco's savings-box product** and **Mercado Pago's Cofrinho CDB issuer** are unconfirmed.
- **Sicoob** is covered by FGCoop per cooperative, not by FGC; the datasets need a coverage kind.
- **Lock-in polarity**: research assumed 5 = least locked-in; the dataset schema must fix this.

## Flags for the architect/owner before this goes into a Zod-validated data file

1. **ISPB/COMPE for all four** — sourced from aggregator sites, not a direct Bacen fetch (Bacen's own PDF/CSV would not render as text through the available fetch tool). High confidence given cross-source agreement, but worth one direct confirmation pass.

## Key finding per bank (the "tricky" question)

- **XP**: money in the XP "conta digital" and the CDBs shown as "renda fixa" investments are
  issued by **Banco XP S.A.** (ISPB 33264668, COMPE 348), a full multiple bank and FGC
  participant. The brokerage side (order routing, custody of third-party securities) sits in a
  different legal entity, **XP Investimentos CCTVM S.A.** (ISPB 02332886, COMPE 102). XP's own
  support content says the conta digital balance "vai render automaticamente como se fosse uma
  aplicação" but the exact instrument/rate for that automatic yield could not be confirmed via
  direct fetch (403) — **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** for the precise default rate; headline
  CDB-with-daily-liquidity offers seen range 100%–230% CDI depending on campaign/tranche, so no
  single canonical number exists to publish.
- **Sicoob**: there is no single "Sicoob S.A." — the customer opens an account as a **member
  (cooperado)** of one specific **cooperativa singular** (e.g. Sicoob Credsaopaulo, Sicoob
  Coopemg …), each its own CNPJ/legal entity, clearing through **Banco Cooperativo Sicoob S.A.**
  ("Bancoob", ISPB 02038232, COMPE 756) for payments. Sicoob's savings product is a genuine
  regulated **caderneta de poupança**, not a CDB wrapper. Coverage is **FGCoop**, not FGC, and —
  important nuance for reserve-headroom math — FGCoop's R$250k ordinary guarantee is applied
  **per associated institution** (i.e., per cooperativa singular), not per the whole Sicoob
  brand/system, unlike FGC's per-conglomerate rule. Membership requires buying cooperative
  quotas (**capital social/cota-parte**) and exiting/redeeming that capital is friction-heavy
  (only after fiscal-year-end net-asset assessment) — a real lock-in mechanic specific to the
  cooperative model.
- **PicPay**: two separate legal entities, confirmed via PicPay's own help-center article
  explaining why users get two separate annual tax statements. **PicPay Serviços S.A.
  Instituição de Pagamento** (CNPJ 22.896.431/0001-10) holds the everyday payment-account balance
  and is _not_ itself a deposit-taking bank. **PicPay Bank Banco Múltiplo S.A.** (CNPJ
  09.516.419/0001-75, ISPB 09516419, COMPE 079) is the licensed bank that issues the CDBs behind
  both the "cofrinho"/automatic account yield (102% CDI, since ~10 Jul 2025 wrapped as CDB with a
  rolling 30-day cycle per new deposit) and the explicit CDB investment product. Multiple
  sources (PicPay's own help center + press) state this yield **is** FGC-covered, because it is
  legally a CDB issued by a bank, unlike Mercado Pago's default balance treatment below. PicPay
  Bank absorbed former **Banco Original do Agronegócio** operations/license.
- **Mercado Pago**: the opposite structure from PicPay on the default balance. Mercado Pago's own
  blog states explicitly that the **default account balance and its automatic yield (≈100–105%
  CDI) are _not_ FGC-covered** — funds sit in the payment institution
  (**Mercado Pago Instituição de Pagamento Ltda.**, CNPJ 10.573.521/0001-91, ISPB 10573521, COMPE 323) and are backed instead by segregated Federal Government Bonds / a dedicated Central Bank
  account, per payment-institution safeguarding rules (this is the standard IP-not-a-bank
  segregation regime, not a Mercado Pago-specific guarantee). Only the separate, opt-in
  "Cofrinho" CDB product (100–140% CDI depending on campaign/Meli+ subscription) carries FGC
  coverage. **The exact legal entity that issues that CDB was not confirmed** — Mercado Pago's
  Brazilian prudential conglomerate includes **Mercado Crédito Sociedade de Crédito, Financiamento
  e Investimento S.A. (SCFI)**, but a financeira/SCFI is legally restricted to issuing RDB
  (Recibo de Depósito Bancário), not CDB, in Brazilian regulation; it's therefore unclear from
  the sources found whether the "CDB" label is precise or the paper is actually placed with a
  third-party partner bank. **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE — flag for a human reviewer to check Mercado
  Pago's official Cofrinho regulamento/prospectus (not found in this pass) before publishing an
  issuer name.**

## Gaps to close before merge (explicit flags)

1. **XP conta digital automatic-yield mechanics** — XP's own support article title claims the
   balance yields automatically, but a third-party comparison site claims XP does _not_ auto-yield
   idle balances (unlike Nubank/PicPay). These two claims conflict and neither was confirmable via
   direct fetch (403 on `atendimento.xpi.com.br`). Needs a human to open the article directly or
   check the current XP app.
2. **Mercado Pago Cofrinho CDB issuer** — not named in any source found. `Mercado Crédito SCFI`
   is the group's licensed credit vehicle but SCFIs are legally restricted to RDB, not CDB, in
   Brazil, so the issuer is genuinely unclear from public content. Needs the Cofrinho
   regulamento/prospectus or a Bacen/CVM filing.
3. **FGC/FGCoop primary-source confirmation** — neither `fgc.org.br/instituicoes-associadas-e-conglomerados`
   nor `fgcoop.coop.br` rendered readable content to this session's fetch tool (both are JS SPAs).
   All FGC/FGCoop participation claims above rest on secondary sources (bank/broker content,
   financial-education sites). Given ADR-0006's blocking requirement for cited evidence, this is
   the single biggest gap — recommend a manual pull of the FGC/FGCoop consultation tools (or their
   underlying API, gated behind mTLS + API keys per FGC's technical manuals) before merge.
4. **Open Finance directory brand entries** — not independently pulled for any of the four
   institutions from `data.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/participants`; only asserted from
   secondary mentions.
5. **App-store ratings** — gathered for Sicoob only (App Store 4.9); XP, PicPay and Mercado Pago
   app-quality scores are "insufficient evidence."
6. **Security criterion** — only Mercado Pago got substantive research (2FA mechanics +
   complaint friction). XP, Sicoob and PicPay security posture is "insufficient evidence" and
   needs a dedicated pass (biometric auth, device-binding, fraud-liability policy, breach history).
7. **Fees criterion** — thin across all four institutions; none has a full, itemized fee schedule
   in this file. Needs a dedicated pass against each institution's official tarifário.
8. **consumidor.gov.br indices** — not obtained for any of the four institutions;
   `consumidor.gov.br` did not return usable scores via search snippets in this pass (it's also a
   JS-heavy site). Needs a direct/manual pull.
