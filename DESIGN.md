## Direction

A calm, trustworthy finance tool for a couple. Not a fintech landing page. Density and hierarchy
borrowed from good dashboard and banking patterns; nothing copied.

## Formatting (pt-BR)

- Currency: `R$ 1.234,56`; negative amounts as `-R$ 1.234,56`.
- Dates: `02/09/2026`; month labels as `set/2026`.
- Numbers: `.` thousands, `,` decimals. Percentages: `12,5%`.
- Time zone: the household's, default `America/Sao_Paulo`.
